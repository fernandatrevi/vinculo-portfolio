01 / Motivation

Information may be fragmented. The goal is to connect it.

Vínculo is designed around the opportunity to connect nonprofit discovery with administrative tools. This motivates the prototype; no operational improvements at real organizations have been measured yet.

04 / Transparency

What is implemented — and what remains

Vínculo is not yet an operational financial platform. Donation entries are manual records; the system does not process cards or payments. The prototype uses fictional data and needs security, permission and workflow validation before working with real donors.
